BlossomPricer FAQ

What is BlossomPricer?
BlossomPricer is a community run website for pricing items across the four BlossomCraft sub servers. Prices are set by handpicked site staff, who are experienced players from all four servers.

Are prices always accurate?
Our site staff try to price items according to what other players are selling them for, while taking into account that certain sales (such as rushed sales) may not reflect actual item prices. In addition, if an item that is part of an exotic craft (such as a genesis orb) is being sold for a certain price, but the price of the item it is crafted into does not reflect the individual item prices, the crafted item’s price takes precedent and the items used to craft it will have their prices adjusted accordingly.

That being said, prices will change, and a certain price or price range may no longer be accurate in a short period of time. You should check the timestamp of the price, and if a newer item has a month old price, you can assume that it may not be accurate anymore.

Does BlossomPricer show “true” prices?
No, it does not. To put it simply, there is no way to have "true" prices, as they will always change. The prices shown are strictly recommendations from the site staff, and, as stated above, are not always accurate, especially if they are old.

What do I do if I think a price needs to be changed or set?
We are always open to feedback on prices. If you haven't already, log in using Discord. Then, you can press the "Request Price Change/Set" button on any item page. A notification will be sent to staff within 24 hours showing a summary of these requests.

How can I help with pricing?
You can apply for site staff on the BlossomCraft Discord marketplace post. Search for "BlossomPricer" to find it. We specifically look for experienced and active players, but there is no item quota or major time commitment involved.
